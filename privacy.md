# Questic Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 26, 2026_

Questic is a goals app with a friendly companion. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

Everything you put into Questic stays on your iPhone. We don't collect it, see it, sell it or share it.

## What Questic stores, and where

Questic saves the following **only on your device**:

- your goals, steps, and when you finished them
- the names you choose for yourself and your friend
- your chats with your friend (these are written by the app itself; nothing is sent to an AI service)
- the souvenirs and treasures you've collected
- your settings, such as when your day starts

Questic has no account, no sign-in and no servers of its own.

## Planning help from Apple Intelligence (optional)

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, your friend can suggest small steps for a goal. Questic asks you first, and it stays off unless you say yes. You can switch it off any time in Questic › Settings › Your data › "Plan with" your friend (for example "Plan with Moss").

When you ask for ideas, Apple's language model reads the goal's name, your "why", your answers to two questions and the goal's existing steps. It runs **entirely on your iPhone**: nothing is sent to Questic, to Apple or to anyone else. Suggestions are only suggestions. Nothing is added to your goals unless you pick it.

## iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, Questic keeps a copy of your goals and your friend's name in **your own private iCloud storage**, so they come back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling. This storage belongs to your Apple Account: we can't see it, and Apple's privacy policy covers it. You can turn this off in iPhone Settings › [your name] › iCloud.

## What Questic does not do

- It does not collect personal information.
- It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
- It does not share data with third parties.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, Questic schedules reminders on your iPhone, such as a nudge from your friend or a timer finishing. They are created and delivered by your device. You can turn them off at any time in Questic › Settings › Reminders and notifications, or in iPhone Settings › Notifications › Questic.

## Backups and deleting your data

Your Questic data is included in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud or computer), which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

You can delete everything at any time in Questic › Settings › Delete all my data. This also removes the copy in your iCloud. Deleting the app removes the data on your iPhone; the iCloud copy can be removed in iPhone Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.

## Children

Questic collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and describe the change in the app's release notes.
