# Questic Support

Questic is a goals app with a friendly companion. Pick Moss the crocodile or Hoot the owl, write your own small steps, and your friend cheers you on every day.

## Contact

Email **arnavsomani1607@gmail.com**. Tell us your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

## Common questions

**Where is my data?**
Everything stays on your iPhone. Questic has no account and no servers. See the [privacy policy](../privacy/).

**How do I delete everything?**
In Questic, go to Settings › Your data › Delete all my data. Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone.

**Why don't I see "Plan with Moss" (or "Plan with Hoot")?**
Step ideas use Apple Intelligence, which needs an iPhone that supports it with Apple Intelligence switched on in the Settings app. Questic asks before using it the first time. If you said "No thanks", you can switch it back on in Questic › Settings › Your data.

**How long can a sprint be?**
Up to 3 months. For something bigger, make it a journey: choose how many days a week you'll work on it, from once a week to every day.

**I missed a day. Did I lose my streak?**
You get one free miss each week, and unfinished steps simply move to the next day. Your friend never shames you.

**How do reminders work?**
Your friend sends at most one friendly notification a day. Turn them on or off in iPhone Settings › Notifications › Questic.
